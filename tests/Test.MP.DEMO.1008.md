A demo long-running test. While it runs, the console shows its log in a box with a progress header: the
stages, a bar of the items done, the items running and an error. It then embeds a small HTML report, which
opens from **Reports** in the report's left menu and from this result.

It runs with `Invoke-Maester -IncludeLongRunning`, or `Invoke-Maester -Pack DEMO`.
